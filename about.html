About Us

Interlab System Technologies, LLC is a medical and chemical supply distribution company located in Nigeria. Founded by Olanrewaju Sanni in 2014, Interlab System Technologies connects customers within the healthcare industry with high quality supplies.

Interlab System Technologies serves as a one-stop shop in that the Company connects Nigeria Healthcare providers with medical equipment and supplies. The Company provides high quality supplies, services and support, specializing in laboratory equipment, chemicals and reagents, diagnostic reagents and kits, medical safety equipment, along with molecular and microbiology products.

The Company operates a Nigerian warehouse that houses equipment and supplies that are in high demand. Quarterly supply catalogs detailing a wide range of products is available upon request.

Interlab System Technologies offers much needed medical supplies to customers in Nigeria at discounted prices with the customer’s best interest in mind. The Company is committed to bridging the gap between the developed and developing economies by helping the science industry embrace new technologies through the supply of its products.



Our Mission Statement

Interlab System Technologies strives to become the leading medical and chemical supply distributor withing Nigeria thorough unparalled prices, integrity, courtesy, transparency, superior customer service and a voluminous collection.
